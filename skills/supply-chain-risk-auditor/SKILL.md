# Supply-chain risk auditing

Use for dependency and toolchain risk assessment, especially before accepting
MCP servers, plugins, packages, or provider integrations. Inventory direct and
transitive dependencies, identify provenance and maintainers, check maintenance
and security-contact signals, and distinguish risk assessment from vulnerability
scanning and license review. Cite current evidence for externally sourced
claims and never install or replace dependencies implicitly.
